Live Local Act · Miami Beach

14 Live Local projects. No public hearing required.

Florida's Live Local Act lets developers build apartments on commercial land at the city's highest allowed density and up to the tallest height allowed within a mile, if at least 40% of the rental units are affordable. In Miami Beach, the Planning Director approves these plans administratively, without a public hearing or Commission vote. Here is every application on file.
