Privacy

How this site handles the information you share with us.

What we collect

When you sign up to stay informed, we collect your first and last name, your email address and, if you choose, your mobile number. We don’t collect anything else through this site, and we don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use your email address to send updates from David about Miami Beach. We use your mobile number only if you check the box to get text messages, and only to send those updates.

Text messages

Text messages are optional and not required to sign up. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to stop getting texts, or HELP for help. We don’t sell, rent or share mobile numbers or text-message consent with anyone else for their marketing.

Who handles your information

Sign-ups are passed through a secure form service hosted by Cloudflare and stored with iContact, our email service. They handle your information only to deliver our messages. We don’t sell your information.

Your browser

This site saves a few settings in your own browser, such as your language, light or dark display, and whether you closed the sign-up window. They never leave your device.

Unsubscribing

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also email the office and ask us to remove your information.

Florida public records

Under Florida law, email addresses are public records. If you don’t want your email address released in response to a public records request, don’t send electronic mail to this entity. Instead, contact the office by phone or in writing.
