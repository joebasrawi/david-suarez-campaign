December 10–16, 2026

G20 traffic guide

Collins Avenue · 44th to 47th streets

You can still drive north and south. The usual northbound lanes will close. Both directions will use the southbound lanes.

Check the City’s latest updates ↗

The summit takes place in Doral on December 14–15. Allow extra time throughout Miami Beach starting Thursday, December 10. Expect delays and follow police directions.

Parking and walking

Curbside parking on this stretch of Collins and the 46th Street municipal lot (P71) will close. The Beachwalk will also close between 44th and 47th streets. Follow signs for the alternate pedestrian path.

Trolleys and buses

The free city trolley will keep running. Routes serving Collins Avenue, 41st Street and Alton Road may detour. Check the trolley tracker or GO Miami-Dade Transit app before leaving.

Get alerts

Text MBAlert to 888777 for traffic and emergency alerts. Restrictions can change on short notice.
