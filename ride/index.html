Proposed Miami Beach law

Under 15? You could lose your e-bike.

The City Commission is considering a law that would ban anyone under 15 from riding e-bikes and e-scooters in Miami Beach, and make every other rider carry ID.

Speak up before the final vote ↘

14 and under

No riding.

You couldn't ride an e-bike, e-scooter or any other motorized scooter on any street, sidewalk or bike lane in the city.

15 and up

Carry ID, every ride.

You'd need a photo ID on you whenever you ride. A Florida school ID with your name, photo and date of birth counts.

Pedal bikes

Not affected.

Regular bikes with no motor, and wheelchairs or other mobility aids, are not covered.

For parents

The proposal is item R5 H, "Regulation of Micromobility Devices," sponsored by Commissioner Tanya K. Bhatt. It would add Section 70-75 to the City Code.

A rider breaking it could get a ticket from police or a park ranger. It is a noncriminal traffic infraction, with fines set by state law (F.S. 316.655). The rules would apply on public streets, sidewalks, bike lanes and alleys, not on private property.

Florida law allowed cities to set these age and ID limits in 2025. Doral set a minimum age of 15, and the City of Miami set 16.
