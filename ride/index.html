The Commission wants your e-⁠bike/⁠scooter.

A proposal at City Hall would ban anyone under 15 from riding e-bikes and e-scooters in Miami Beach, and make every other rider carry ID.

It isn't law yet. It was sent to the Public Safety committee, and you can help stop it there.

Help stop it ↘What it would do

What the proposal would do

14 and under

Banned from riding.

No e-bike, e-scooter or other motorized scooter on any street, sidewalk or bike lane in the city.

15 and up

ID every ride.

You'd have to carry a photo ID whenever you ride. A Florida school ID with your name, photo and date of birth counts.

Break it

Get a ticket.

Police and park rangers could write you up. It's a traffic infraction with fines set by state law.

Regular pedal bikes, wheelchairs and other mobility aids would not be affected.

For parents

The details

The proposal is item R5 H, "Regulation of Micromobility Devices," sponsored by Commissioner Tanya K. Bhatt. It would add Section 70-75 to the City Code.

A rider breaking it could get a ticket from police or a park ranger. It is a noncriminal traffic infraction, with fines set by state law (F.S. 316.655). The rules would apply on public streets, sidewalks, bike lanes and alleys, not on private property.

Florida law allowed cities to set these age and ID limits in 2025. Doral set a minimum age of 15, and the City of Miami set 16.
