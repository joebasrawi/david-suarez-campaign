How updates work

The publishing workflow is scheduled to check public feeds every six hours. New agendas, approved minutes, videos and City updates appear after a successful refresh and publication.

A “last checked” date means the source was retrieved. It does not mean the underlying record was newly published. If a source fails, the last saved snapshot stays available.

Three different kinds of content

City records: official agendas, approved minutes, registries, announcements and GIS project data.

Navigation aids: searchable titles, extracted vote labels and resident guides. These help you find a source; they are not legal findings.

David’s perspective: his videos and social posts, presented as commentary from the commissioner.

Important limitations

Agenda short titles and sponsorship metadata come through the third-party Miami Beach Agenda navigator. Vote labels are extracted from approved minutes and may not capture every motion. “No amendment identified” is not proof that no language changed. Project map points may represent the center of a larger work area. Always consult the linked official record before relying on a summary.

Privacy & accessibility

Site-wide searches run in your browser. Optional address lookup sends the submitted address to Esri. This site has no signup database or advertising trackers. Loading a YouTube player connects to YouTube; project-map tiles connect to OpenStreetMap. Fonts and video thumbnails also load from external services.

We aim for keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, readable contrast and reduced-motion support. If something prevents you from using the site, please tell the office which page and device were affected.

Something doesn’t look right?

Send the page link, meeting date and item number if applicable. Corrections should be checked against the underlying City document.
